Locked In

A machined aluminum phone lockbox for focused work

Exploded view with dimensions (all measurements in mm)

Concept

Original concept sketch

A precision-machined aluminum box that holds your phone. No locks, no timers, no apps. Just enough friction to make you think twice before reaching for it.

The lid attaches with N52 neodymium magnets, strong enough to require intentional effort to open. The satisfying snap when closing creates a psychological commitment to focus.

Sizing Rationale

Target device: iPhone 16 Pro Max (largest current iPhone)

Bare phone: 163 × 77.6 × 8.25 mm
Phone with thin case: ~168 × 82 × 11 mm
Phone with medium case: ~170 × 84 × 13 mm
Pocket size: 175 × 88 × 16 mm

Clearance (medium case): 5mm on length, 4mm on width, 3mm on depth — fits most cases except heavy-duty rugged cases like OtterBox Defender.

The 12mm wall thickness provides structural rigidity and enough material for the Ø8.2mm magnet pockets in the corners without compromising the rim.

Machining Process

Base (2 setups)

Face mill top surface of billet
Rough phone pocket 175×88×16mm with 1/4" 2-flute end mill, leave 0.5mm stock
Finish pocket walls and floor, 3.2mm corner radius from tool geometry
Drill 4× magnet pockets: Ø8.2mm × 3.2mm deep
Flip part, fixture in soft jaws (pocket side down)
Profile exterior contour with 1/4" 3-flute end mill
Add 3mm corner fillets on vertical edges
Add 1mm edge breaks on bottom perimeter

Lid (2–3 setups)

Face both sides to 10mm final thickness
Drill 4× magnet pockets from bottom face: Ø8.2mm × 3.2mm deep
Profile exterior contour + 3mm corner fillets
Add 1mm edge breaks on top and bottom perimeters

Finishing

Deburr all edges by hand
Bead blast for uniform matte texture
Brush finish (linear grain) for premium look
Optional: Type II anodize (clear or color)

Magnet Assembly

Important: Neodymium magnets are brittle ceramic — do NOT press fit. Use slip fit pockets (8.2mm for 8mm magnets) and secure with CA glue or epoxy.

Mark polarity before separating magnets. Base magnets face North up; lid magnets face South down. Test attraction before gluing.

Recommended magnets: Hailson 8×3mm N52 on Amazon

3D Printing Notes

For prototyping, print in PLA or PETG. Use 8.0mm magnet holes (not 8.2mm) to account for plastic shrinkage — this gives a friction fit without glue for testing.
